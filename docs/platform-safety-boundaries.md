# Platform Safety Boundaries

Freedom World does not prohibit speech because it is controversial. It prohibits conduct that directly causes harm.

## Restricted conduct

- direct threats
- stalking and targeted harassment
- doxxing
- account takeover
- fraud
- malware
- child sexual exploitation
- exploitation of vulnerable users
- direct incitement to violence
- coordinated abuse against a person or group

## Distinction

The system evaluates:
- content in context
- intent and impact
- directness of harm
- likelihood of real-world harm

The system does not evaluate:
- whether the idea is politically correct
- whether the viewpoint is mainstream
- whether the user is aligned with platform ideology
